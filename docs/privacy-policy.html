Privacy Policy — Formula

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Formula ("Formula," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Influenceish LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Formula mobile app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Formula, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact information — your email address, the name you provide, and your TikTok handle. Optionally, a phone number (e.g. for WhatsApp updates).
  • Content you create and analyze — the scripts Formula generates for you, and information derived from the public TikTok videos you ask Formula to analyze. We do not collect private TikTok account credentials.
  • Identifiers — a user account ID, and a device push token if you enable notifications.
  • Purchase information — records of in-app purchases and subscriptions (handled through Apple and our payments partner RevenueCat). We never receive or store your full payment card details.

We do not collect health, financial account, precise location, browsing history, or contacts data.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: building your creator brain, generating scripts, and saving your work.
  • To personalize results to your voice and content.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions, and credits.
  • To send you notifications you've opted into, and account or support messages.
  • To provide customer support and keep the app secure and working.

Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share your data with data brokers for advertising. Formula does not use third-party advertising.

How your information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run Formula, and only as needed: Apple (in-app purchases), RevenueCat (subscriptions and purchase validation), our backend (Influenceish IQ) for content analysis and script generation, and push notification services. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Notifications — you control WhatsApp, SMS, push, and email opt-ins in the app and your device settings.
  • Access or deletion — request a copy of your data, or deletion of your account and data, by emailing us below.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

Children

Formula is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here and change the "Last updated" date above.
