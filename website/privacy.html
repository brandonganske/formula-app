Privacy Policy.

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Formula (“Formula,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by Influenceish LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Formula mobile app and website collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Formula, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the app:

  • Account & contact information — your email address, the name you provide, and your TikTok handle. If you choose to, you may also provide a phone number (for example, for WhatsApp updates). Providing a phone number is optional.
  • Content you create and analyze — the scripts Formula generates for you, and information derived from the public TikTok videos you ask Formula to analyze (for example, your account’s content, or a video you paste in to rewrite). We do not collect private TikTok account credentials.
  • Identifiers — a user account ID, and a device push token if you enable notifications.
  • Purchase information — records of in-app purchases and subscriptions (handled through Apple and our payments partner RevenueCat). We never receive or store your full payment card details; those are handled by Apple.

We do not collect health, financial account, precise location, browsing history, or contacts data.

TikTok data

When you connect your TikTok account or handle, Formula analyzes your public TikTok content — videos, captions, and public profile stats — to build your creator profile. We access only what TikTok’s APIs permit with your authorization, and we never see or store your TikTok password. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time and request deletion of the data we derived from it.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: building your “creator brain,” generating scripts, and saving your work.
  • To personalize results to your voice and content.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions, and credits.
  • To send you notifications you’ve opted into (for example, when your analysis is ready), and account or support messages.
  • To provide customer support and keep the app secure and working.

Tracking

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not share your data with data brokers for advertising. Formula does not use third-party advertising.

How your information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run Formula, and only as needed:

  • Apple — for in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • RevenueCat — to manage subscriptions, credits, and purchase validation.
  • Our backend (Influenceish IQ) — to analyze content and generate scripts.
  • Push notification services — to deliver notifications you’ve enabled.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can delete your account and its associated data directly in the app under Profile → Account, or request deletion at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Your choices and rights

  • Notifications — you control WhatsApp, SMS, push, and email opt-ins in the app and your device settings.
  • Access or deletion — you may request a copy of your data, or delete your account in-app (Profile → Account) or by emailing us at the address below.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to certain processing.

Children

Formula is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We’ll post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date above.

Contact us
